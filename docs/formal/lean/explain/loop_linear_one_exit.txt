 loop_linear_one_exit
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
The RUE-1614 shape inside a loop: a linear binding is dropped before one break
and kept at the other. The loop's outgoing state is §5.5's join over its
exits, which is undefined on a linear binding that is MovedOut at one and
Owned at the other (E0443). The run takes the exit that keeps it, and the
let's scope exit then meets a live linear value.

Rules exercised: (Loop-Break) §5.7 · (Break) §5.7 · §5.5 join · 3.8:50 · 3.8:80
