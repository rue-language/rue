 two_params_dropped_at_pop
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Two by-value destructor-bearing arguments of two types the callee never
consumes: the frame pop drops the last parameter first, so the trace is 2 and
its field's 3, then 1, then the value 0.
