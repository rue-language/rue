 enum_call_scrutinee
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A call in scrutinee position: the enum comes back across a frame boundary and
(D-Match) binds its payload in the caller's frame, so the payload's drop is
owed to the caller's arm and not to the callee's pop.

Rules exercised: (Match) §5.5 · (Call) §5.8 · (D-Call) §6.9 · (D-Match) §6.6
