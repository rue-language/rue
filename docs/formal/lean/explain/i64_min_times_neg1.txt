min_T * -1 at i64: an overflow trap, because -min_T is one past max_T. The
bridge found the compiler wrapping this one and exiting 0: it lowered a
checked multiply by a constant power of two as a left shift checked by
shifting back, and took the bit pattern of min_T, 2^63, for one (RUE-2318).
The compiler traps now, and the case stays as the regression signal.
