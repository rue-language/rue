 loop_moved_prev_iteration
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A loop whose body drops an outer affine binding and completes, so the back
edge leaves it MovedOut, the loop-head state is the join of entry and back
edge, MovedOut, and the @drop is refused at that head: moved in a previous
iteration (3.8:79; the compiler reports E0205). The break-less loop is the
let's whole body, so nothing follows it. The machine drops it on the first
turn and meets the moved-out cell on the second.

Rules exercised: (Loop-Div) §5.7 · 3.8:79 · (@Drop) §5.3 · (D-Loop-Iter) §6.10
