RUE-1615's shape one loop up: the inner loop drops an outer affine binding and
breaks, so it reaches no back edge, but the outer loop does, and at the outer
head the binding is MovedOut, where the inner @drop is refused (3.8:79; the
compiler reports E0205). The machine drops it on the first outer turn and
meets the moved-out cell on the second.

Rules exercised: (Loop-Break) §5.7 · 3.8:79 · (@Drop) §5.3 · (D-Loop-Iter) §6.10 · (D-Break) §6.10
